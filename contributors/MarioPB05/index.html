Full Stack Developer · SAFA, Seville · @MarioPB05

Hey! I'm Mario, a full stack developer from Olivares, a small town just outside Seville in the south of Spain. I got into this the way a lot of people do: messing about with online tutorials as a kid, breaking things, and eventually realising I'd rather be doing that than anything else. So I went and studied it properly.

These days I build web and mobile apps for SAFA, a network of schools across Andalusia. Most of my time goes into Itacapp, which somewhere between 1,300 and 1,500 students, parents and teachers open every single day. Shipping to real people who will absolutely tell you when something is broken has taught me more than any tutorial ever did.

Things I've built

Itacapp

A cross-platform app for the SAFA school network: grades, attendance, weekly schedules, notifications, and parents managing several kids from one account. Ionic + Angular on the front, a custom PHP API on the back, Firebase for auth and push. I joined during my internship and now I'm the one keeping it alive and growing it.

The bits I'm proudest of: the whole authentication flow, a personalised push notification system, and a real-time chat between parents and tutors that people actually use. The teacher module went out recently with timetables, duties and academic follow-up.

Ionic Angular PHP Firebase

Colette's Shop

A gamified online store inspired by Brawl Stars, built with Symfony. You buy random boxes, unlock brawlers, earn gems towards discounts, set your favourite brawler as your avatar, and leave reviews. There's an admin dashboard behind it for boxes, orders and stats, plus music and sound effects because it felt wrong without them. A school project I had far too much fun with.

Symfony PHP MySQL
