A little demo from this template

This directory template ships with a live Metropolis–Hastings sampler exploring a 2D mixture-of-Gaussians target distribution — the walker proposes a random step, accepts it with probability min(1, p(x')/p(x)), and rejects otherwise. Warmer cells in the heatmap mean the chain has spent more time there, which is exactly where the true density is highest. Kept here as a fun example of what you can embed on your own page.
