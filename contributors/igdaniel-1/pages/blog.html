Pulse Foundry x ABI Frameworks Healthcare Data Hackthon
written by: India Grace Daniel
June 28, 2026

The Pulse Foundry x ABI Frameworks Healthcare Data Hackthon brought together over 100 builders and software engineers to solve the problem of healthcare approval backlogs.

ABI Frameworks is live in production on PointClickCare across skilled nursing facilities, structuring complete patient profiles from electronic health records and delivering them to the vendors who depend on that data for care delivery, eligibility determination, and missed-billable detection. We put that messy, high-stakes challenge directly in front of our builders fragmented patient data, eligibility logic, record deduplication, and the billable events that fall through the cracks today.

Working on this project was an invaluable experience. This was my first time leading a dual language Chinese-English team in an integrated AI envoroment.

We built our Python ETL pipeline in Codex to process patient information sourced from a PointClickCare API. We then wired up a React interface leveraging Claude Code to quickly scaffold the project, freeing valuable hackthon time to polish our final UI.

Check out the Pulse Foundry's Mitchell Itkin's coverage of the event here! (look out for me in the left corner!!)
